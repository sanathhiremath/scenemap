# Generated fixture rights

The PNG frames under `artifacts/frames/` are original deterministic graphics generated by this project. They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

They depict geometric markers rather than real people or real camera scenes. Their purpose is reproducible geometry testing. This dedication covers generated images, not third-party dependency packages or model weights. SceneMap downloads no model weights.
